==============================================================
  PASOORIIZM — HOW TO INSTALL & RUN (STEP-BY-STEP)
==============================================================

--- ONE-TIME SETUP ---

1. Double-click "setup.bat".
2. Wait until you see "SETUP COMPLETE! [OK]" in the window.
3. Press any key to close the setup window.


--- HOW TO RUN THE APP ---

1. Double-click "Pasooriizm.bat".
2. The app will open in your web browser automatically at http://localhost:3000.
3. Paste any Spotify playlist/song link and click Download.
4. When finished, press any key inside the "Pasooriizm.bat" window to stop the app.


--- NOTE ---

- No administrator privileges required.
- Everything (Node.js, yt-dlp, ffmpeg) is automatically managed.
- If the browser does not open automatically: Open http://localhost:3000 manually.
